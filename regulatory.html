Regulatory & Compliance

Regulatory strategy and documentation for medical devices and medical software.

AVIORA provides regulatory and compliance support across product classification, technical documentation, risk management, quality-system processes and submission preparation. Scope is tailored to the device, software and target jurisdiction.

Regulatory services

Structured support from classification to market entry.

Product Classification

Assess the intended use, technology and risk profile to help determine the applicable regulatory pathway.

Regulatory Strategy

Develop a market-specific regulatory plan for India and, where applicable, international markets.

Technical Documentation

Prepare or support technical documentation, product specifications, traceability and evidence packages.

Risk Management

Support risk-management files and linkage between hazards, controls, verification and residual risk.

Software / SaMD Compliance

Regulatory documentation for software lifecycle, cybersecurity, usability and software verification activities.

Submission Preparation

Organise submission-ready documentation and support interactions required for the selected regulatory pathway.

Quality framework

Documentation should reflect the product—not a generic template.

Medical-device compliance depends on the product's intended use, risk classification, technology, evidence and market. AVIORA's approach is to map the documentation set to those product-specific requirements and maintain traceability across the development lifecycle.
