AI & SaMD

Intelligent medical software, developed with clinical and regulatory requirements in mind.

AVIORA supports the development of AI-enabled medical software and Software as a Medical Device (SaMD), from product definition and software architecture through risk management, verification and regulatory-ready documentation.

Development capabilities

Build the software around the medical use case.

Clinical Use Definition

Intended use, user needs, clinical workflow and software functionality defined before development decisions are locked.

AI / ML Development

AI model development and integration for appropriate medical use cases, with traceability between requirements, data and outputs.

Medical Image Analysis

Software concepts for analysis of medical images and other structured clinical data, subject to the intended use and evidence requirements.

Risk Management

Identification and control of software, clinical and use-related risks throughout the product lifecycle.

Verification & Validation

Test strategy, traceability and evidence planning to demonstrate that the software meets defined requirements.

Cybersecurity & Usability

Support for security, user-interface and human-factors considerations relevant to the software product.

Regulatory-aware development

Software development and regulatory documentation should evolve together.

For medical software, product requirements, risk controls, verification evidence and technical documentation are closely connected. AVIORA can structure the development workflow so these deliverables are traceable rather than assembled only at the end of a project.

Typical lifecycle

01  Product concept & intended use
02  Software requirements & architecture
03  Risk management & cybersecurity
04  Implementation / AI model development
05  Verification & validation
06  Technical documentation & regulatory preparation
